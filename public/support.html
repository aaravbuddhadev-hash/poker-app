Support

Last updated 16 November 2026

Thanks for using Riverline. Here's how to get help.

Report a problem or ask a question: open a support request on GitHub (a free GitHub account is needed). Describe what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened, plus your device and iOS version if you're using the app.

Support requests there are public, so please don't include your email address, passwords or session details.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Without one, everything stays on your device. An account backs up your data and syncs it between devices.

I forgot my password. On the sign-in form, tap Forgot password? and follow the emailed link. You'll set a new password on the Riverline website, and it then works in the app too.

How do I delete my account? Profile → Account → Delete account. It's immediate and permanent, and removes all of your data. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I get my data out? Profile → Your data → Export CSV creates a spreadsheet of all your sessions.

Does Riverline involve real money? No. It records results you type in and teaches the game; it never handles money or links to gambling.

Gambling help

If gambling is causing problems for you or someone you know: in the US, call or text 1-800-GAMBLER; elsewhere, visit gamblingtherapy.org.

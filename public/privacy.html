Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

Riverline is a poker companion for recording your own session results and learning Texas Hold'em, available as a website and an iPhone app. This policy explains what information Riverline handles, why, and the choices you have. In short: no ads, no tracking, no selling of data, and you can use Riverline without an account.

Without an account

If you don't create an account, everything you enter (sessions, notes, settings, lesson and quiz progress, puzzle answers) is saved only on your device, in the app's local storage. It is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app, or clearing the website's data in your browser, removes it.

With an account

Accounts are optional and exist to back up your data and sync it between your devices. When you create one, Riverline stores:

InformationWhy
Your email address and a password (stored only as a secure hash)To sign you in and to send password-reset emails you request
A random account IDTo keep your records separate from everyone else's
Your sessions: date, net result, any starting and ending amounts, duration, game type and notesTo show your records and statistics on all your devices
Your learning progress: completed lessons, quiz attempts, daily puzzle answers and which badges you've seenTo keep your progress in step across devices

Amounts are only what you type in yourself. Riverline never connects to bank accounts, gambling sites or payment services, and never handles money.

What Riverline does not collect

No location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, device fingerprints, analytics or usage tracking, and no third-party advertising or analytics tools.

Service providers

  • Supabase hosts the account database and sign-in service for signed-in users (servers in the United States). Each account can only read and change its own records; this is enforced by the database itself. Supabase processes this data only to provide the service.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses when pages are loaded.

Network requests necessarily include your IP address. Riverline does not use it to identify or track you.

How long data is kept

Account data is kept until you delete it. Deleting a session removes it from all your devices. (A small marker without its contents remains, so your other devices know it was deleted.)

Deleting your data

  • Your whole account: in Riverline, open Profile → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything stored with it, immediately.
  • Your sessions only: Profile → Your data → Delete all sessions.
  • This device's copy: sign out. Your data stays in your account and is removed from that device.

You can export your sessions at any time from Profile → Your data → Export CSV.

Sharing

Your data is never sold or shared for advertising. It is only disclosed if required by law.

Children

Riverline is intended for adults and is not directed at children. It is about real-money poker, which is restricted to adults in most places.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are never stored in readable form. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use elsewhere.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy: see the Support page. Please don't post personal information in public support threads; you can delete your account and data yourself at any time, as described above.

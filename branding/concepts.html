Logo concepts

Five marks, each shown full size and as an app icon on dark and light

1 · Card Line

A playing card holding a results line that ends in a gold point. Best fit for Riverline or Stackbook.

2 · Minimal Chip

A clean chip with eight edge spots and a gold spade. The most classic of the five, and instantly reads as poker.

3 · Breakout Spade

A spade cut by a rising line that escapes its edge. Bold and recognizable at small sizes.

4 · The Board

The five community cards drawn as a bar chart, with the river card in gold. Literal and clever for Riverline.

5 · Odds Diamond

A diamond pip framing a percent sign made of two pips. Leans into probability; strongest for Outs.
